FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to the most common questions about our flooring services, process, and pricing.

How long does installation usually take?

Most residential flooring projects take 1–3 days depending on square footage and material.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes — all estimates are 100% free and include a full breakdown of materials and labor.

Do you move furniture before installation?

We can help move light furniture. Larger items may need to be cleared beforehand or arranged in advance.

What types of flooring do you install?

We install hardwood, luxury vinyl plank (LVP), laminate, and select tile flooring systems.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — fully licensed and insured for residential and commercial flooring projects.

Do you handle both residential and commercial jobs?

Yes, we work on homes, offices, rental properties, and commercial spaces.

How do I prepare my home before installation?

Clear small items, valuables, and breakables. We’ll guide you through everything before the project starts.

Can you replace old flooring and dispose of it?

Yes — we handle full removal and disposal of existing flooring when needed.

What affects the price of a flooring project?

Material type, square footage, prep work, and subfloor condition all affect final pricing.
