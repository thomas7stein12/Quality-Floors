WHY CHOOSE US

Flooring craftsmanship you can trust.

We are a privately owned flooring business serving Ooltewah and Collegedale with personalized service, expert installation, and local care on every project. Seventh-Day Adventist owned, we believe quality matters above all and genuinely care about our clients. For over 10 years we've taken satisfaction from seeing floors transform here in America and craft results that make us as proud as our customers are happy.

We started small serving our local area with help from friends and family, and the Lord has blessed us beyond what we imagined. As our client base expands, we now serve Cleveland, Chattanooga, Dalton, Jasper, Dunlap, and sometimes even Knoxville or Atlanta wherever we feel there is a need, no sacrifice is too big when it's done in service of our clients. Wherever the Lord calls us, we're there.

We have been working on floors for decades, our flooring experience begins in Medellín, Colombia. While there we served our clients tirelessly with no lengths being too short to go to give our clients premium flooring transformations. When God called us to move and expand our flooring experience into this wonderful country, we had no plans to lower our work quality, and we strive to improve every day both in our floors as well as our mission. We hope you decide to allow us to work our magic on your floors, magic that was earned through experience.
