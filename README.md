# Quality Floors — Business Website

**Live Demo:** [quality-floors.vercel.app](https://quality-floors.vercel.app/)

A professional, responsive website built for **Quality Floors**, a local flooring business in Collegedale, Tennessee.

This was my first project developed for a real business. The goal was to create a modern online presence that clearly communicates the company's services, establishes credibility with potential customers, and makes it easier for people to learn about the business and get in contact.

* 📱 **Responsive Design** for desktop, tablet, and mobile

## 🎯 Project Goals

The primary goal was to give a small local flooring company a professional online presence that could serve as a first point of contact for potential customers.

The website was designed to:

* Clearly communicate the company's services
* Present the business in a professional way
* Make important information easy to find
* Showcase the company's work
* Improve the customer's first impression of the business
* Provide a foundation for future online growth

---

## 💼 Built for a Real Business

Unlike a practice project, this website was developed around the needs of an actual local business.

I worked with the company's existing identity and services to turn their business information into a customer-facing website.

This required thinking beyond the code itself and considering:

* What information potential customers need
* How to communicate services clearly
* How the business should be presented visually
* How users navigate a business website
* How the experience changes between desktop and mobile

The project gave me practical experience translating real-world business requirements into a working web product.

---

## 📱 Responsive Design

The website was designed to provide a consistent experience across different screen sizes.

Responsive considerations included:

* Flexible page layouts
* Mobile-friendly navigation
* Adaptive typography
* Responsive images
* Flexible spacing and sizing
* Content that scales across desktop and mobile

The goal was to make the business information equally accessible whether a potential customer visits from a computer or a phone.

---

## 🎨 User Experience

The interface was designed around a straightforward business website structure so visitors can quickly understand:

```text id="4v7m48"
Who is Quality Floors?
        ↓
What services do they provide?
        ↓
What does their work look like?
        ↓
How can I contact them?
```

This keeps the site focused on the information a potential customer is most likely looking for.

## 📁 Project Structure

The project uses a lightweight frontend structure designed for maintainability.

├── css/
│   └── ...                # Site styling and responsive layouts
├── js/
│   └── ...                # Client-side functionality
├── images/
│   └── ...                # Business imagery and assets

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Clone the repository

```bash id="s7g4k1"
git clone https://github.com/thomas7stein12/quality-floors.git
cd quality-floors
```

### 2. Open the project

This is a frontend website and does not require a backend server or database to run locally.

Open `index.html` directly in your browser, or use the **Live Server** extension in VS Code.

### 3. Deploy

The production version of the site is deployed through **Vercel**.
